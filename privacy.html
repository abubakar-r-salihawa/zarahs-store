Privacy & support

Zarah's Store is a marketplace connecting shoppers with independent boutiques.

Contact us

For account help, privacy requests or problems using the store, email zarahsstore.Customersupport@gmail.com.

For delivery, refunds or product questions, contact the relevant boutique using the WhatsApp link in My orders. Confirm delivery charges, timing and return conditions with the seller before paying.

Information used by the store

We use the account details you provide, such as your name, email and phone number, to authenticate you and support your account. For orders, we store contact and delivery details, selected products, prices, order status and order history to process and track your purchase requests.

Your device stores your cart, sign-in session and preferences. Authentication is handled by Supabase; the storefront does not store your password in its browser registry.

Who receives information

The store owner and relevant boutique receive the order information needed to manage purchases and delivery. Supabase provides authentication and database services, and Vercel hosts the website. These providers may process technical information necessary to operate their services. External font and image providers receive requests when their resources load.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy terms. Opening a WhatsApp order link passes the order message to WhatsApp; you choose whether to send it. WhatsApp conversations are handled by WhatsApp and the seller.

Payments and permissions

Zarah's Store does not collect card or bank credentials or take online payments. Payment arrangements are made directly with the boutique. The current application does not request your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Retention and deletion

Account and order information is kept while needed to operate your account, fulfil orders and resolve disputes. Some transaction records may need to be retained for applicable legal obligations. We do not promise immediate removal of records that must be retained.

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data from the website or app. Requests are reviewed by the store owner. If you cannot sign in, email the support address above from your registered email address; we may need to verify ownership. We will explain any retained information and the reason when handling your request. Copies of conversations held independently by boutiques or WhatsApp are outside the store's direct control.

Your choices

You may browse without creating an account. You can sign out, clear locally stored cart data, and contact support to request access to or correction of your information. Sign-in is required to place an order.
